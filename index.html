THE PRESS

Load an ink. Then drag the squeegee straight down the screen — slow & firm floods solid ink, fast & loose leaves it streaky. Pull again in a new colour. Let it go a little off-register. That’s the point.

02

THE WALL

One image is never enough. Feed your print into the conveyor and watch it multiply, every cell a different colourway — the Marilyn diptych logic. Hover a tile to knock it off-register.

03

INK LAB

Drag one ink blob onto another on the slab. They smear into a new fluorescent — the Factory names it. Mix it onto the rack and print Warhols in your own colours.
